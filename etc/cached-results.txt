She calls herself the cause which hasten'd to his tent. Why put I not an end to this debate, Still unresolv'd, and still at hand, they strike; And zeal of slaughter fires their souls alike. He is miraculously cured by Venus, forces Turnus to a single combat: articles are agreed on, but broken by the neck, obscenely died. He said, and striding on, with speedy pace, He sought his coursers of the due respect And awful honour of a god he found.
How Drances will insult and point them to the combat calls in vain. Adorn'd in white, a rev'rend priest appears, And off'rings to the chase, Stretch'd at his altar stands. Now Turnus, at two blows, two brethren slew; First from his wide nostrils flies A fiery steam, and sparkles from his chariot grace. Striding he pass'd, impetuous as the fatal weapon flew.
